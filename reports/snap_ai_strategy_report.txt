    4c. User Churn Prediction  —  XGBoost + SMOTE
------------------------------------------------------------
  Rationale: Churn datasets are inherently imbalanced (most users
  are retained). SMOTE generates synthetic minority samples to
  improve recall for at-risk users, which is the critical business
  metric. Threshold tuning further optimises the F1 trade-off
  between precision and recall.

  Algorithm:                          XGBoost Classifier + SMOTE
  Imbalance handling:                 SMOTE (k=5 neighbours)

------------------------------------------------------------
  6. Deployment Architecture
------------------------------------------------------------
  Production deployment would follow a MLOps pipeline: (1) Feature
  Store (Feast/Tecton) for real-time feature serving, (2) Model
  Registry (MLflow) for versioning and A/B gating, (3) Serving layer
  via FastAPI + Kubernetes for low-latency inference, (4) Shadow
  mode deployment before full rollout, (5) Continuous monitoring
  with Evidently AI for data drift, (6) Retraining triggers on KPI
  degradation > 5% over 7-day window.


------------------------------------------------------------
  7. Ethical & Compliance Considerations
------------------------------------------------------------
  All three models are designed with fairness in mind: (a)
  Demographic features are excluded from ad targeting to prevent
  discriminatory ad delivery (per EU AI Act and UK ICO guidelines).
  (b) Churn model outputs are gated through human review before any
  win-back communication is sent to minors (13-17 age group). (c)
  GDPR Article 22 compliance: users can opt out of automated
  decision-making. (d) Model explainability reports (SHAP values)
  are generated quarterly for internal audit.
